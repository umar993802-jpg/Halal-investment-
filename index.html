Frequently Asked Questions

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Investment and trading returns are uncertain and losses are possible, including the full amount invested.

Is this website a Shariah certification?

No. The name Halal Investment does not itself certify any product or service as Shariah-compliant. Request a qualified, independent Shariah review and verify the underlying activities, contracts and instruments.

What do the amounts on this page mean?

They are enquiry placeholders only. Confirm the actual service, fees, terms and legal basis in writing before making any payment.

What should I verify before paying?

Verify the legal entity, relevant registrations, written agreement, fee schedule, custody and withdrawal arrangements, and payment recipient. Never share OTPs, passwords or account credentials.

How can I contact the team?

Use the WhatsApp button to ask general questions. Do not send sensitive financial information.
